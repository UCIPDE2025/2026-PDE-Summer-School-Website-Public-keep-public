Overview

This summer school will feature three weeklong mini-courses led by experts, covering conformally invariant fully nonlinear elliptic equations, Hamilton-Jacobi equations, and incompressible fluids. Participants will also have opportunities to present their work through short talks and poster presentations. The primary goal is to equip graduate students with a strong foundation in key research areas of PDE, foster a sense of community, and encourage future collaborations.
