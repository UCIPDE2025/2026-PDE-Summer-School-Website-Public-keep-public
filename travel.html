Transportation

Airport

The University of California, Irvine is centrally located in Southern California. The most convenient nearby airport is John Wayne Airport (SNA), which is about a 10–15 minute drive from campus. Most hotels in Irvine have shuttle services that can pick you up directly from SNA.

Two alternative airports are Long Beach Airport (LGB), about a 30-minute drive from campus, and Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), about a 50-minute drive away. If arriving from LAX, taxi and shuttle services are available to Irvine, including SuperShuttle.

Campus Map and Parking

The talks will take place in the Interdisciplinary Science and Engineering Building (ISEB) , which you can locate on the campus map (Building 419, Coordinates F5).

The parking area closest to ISEB is Parking Lot 16. Single-day parking permits are available for purchase. For participants from other UC campuses, a parking permit from your home institution may be valid at UC Irvine. Please consult the UC Irvine Visitor Parking information or contact the parking office at (949) 824-7275 for current details.
