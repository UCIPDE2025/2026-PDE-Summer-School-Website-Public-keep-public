Poster Session

2026 UCI PDE Summer School
The poster session provided participants with an opportunity to present their research, discuss their work with speakers and fellow participants, and exchange ideas in an informal setting. A total of 8 participants presented posters during the session.

Poster Competition

As part of the poster session, we held a Best Poster competition. Participants were asked to vote for the poster they thought was the best overall, considering both the mathematical/scientific content and the clarity of the presentation.

Each participant was asked to vote only once, and voting was anonymous.

The two posters receiving the most votes were selected as the winners.

Best Poster Award Winners

Seho Park

University of Wisconsin–Madison
Periodic Homogenization of Hamilton-Jacobi Equations for Infinite Systems of Indistinguishable Particles
